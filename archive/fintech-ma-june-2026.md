# Fintech M&A and Funding Activity — Announced 30 May – 7 June 2026

**Scope:** M&A deals and funding rounds where the **target company** is a technology business in
one of eight sectors:

1. Asset & Wealth Tech
2. Capital Markets Tech
3. Real Estate & Mortgage Tech
4. Banking & Lending Tech
5. Insurtech
6. Payments Tech
7. Financial Info & Analytics
8. Corporate Financial Function (CFO / spend / treasury / accounting tech)

**Inclusion rules:**
- **Announced 30 May – 7 June 2026** (not merely *closed* in that window from an earlier
  announcement).
- **Funding rounds included only if ≥ $20M** (lowered from $25M). M&A included at any size.
- Target must itself be an in-scope **technology** company.

**Compiled:** 7 June 2026. (30–31 May was a weekend and produced no qualifying announcements,
so the live deals all land 1–7 June.)

**Methodology & honesty note:** Every deal below was individually date-verified. Lowering the
floor to $20M did **not** change the qualifying set — the binding constraint in this ~9-day
window is the **announcement date, not deal size.** Three sectors (Asset & Wealth Tech, Capital
Markets Tech, Real Estate & Mortgage Tech) have **no standalone deal announced in-window**; for
each, the nearest qualifying deal (dated, just outside the window) is given so the gap is
actionable. AlphaSense is listed **once**, under its truest home (Financial Info & Analytics),
rather than cross-listed across three sectors.
