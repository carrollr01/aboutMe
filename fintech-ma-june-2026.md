# Fintech M&A and Funding Activity — Announced June 2026

**Scope:** M&A deals and funding rounds where the **target company** operates in one of
seven sectors:

1. Asset & Wealth Tech
2. Capital Markets Tech
3. Real Estate & Mortgage Tech
4. Banking & Lending Tech
5. Insurtech
6. Payments Tech
7. Financial Info & Analytics
8. Corporate Financial Function (CFO / spend / treasury / accounting tech)

**Inclusion rules:**
- Deal must be **announced in June 2026** (not merely *closed/completed* in June from an
  earlier announcement).
- **Funding rounds are included only if > $25M.** M&A is included at any deal size.
- The target company must itself be a **technology** business in one of the sectors above
  (e.g. a traditional life insurer being acquired does not count as insurtech).

**Time window:** Compiled **7 June 2026**, so the effective window is **1–7 June 2026**.
Refresh as the month progresses.

**Methodology:** Web research across deal-tracking and trade press (FinTech Global, FinTech
Futures, Crunchbase News, TechCrunch, BusinessWire/GlobeNewswire/PRNewswire press releases,
sector outlets). Every deal below was individually date-verified to fall inside the window.
A large number of frequently-cited 2026 deals that *surface* in "first week of June" roundups
but were actually **announced in April or May** (or merely closed in June) are listed in the
"Excluded near-misses" section for transparency.

- **Announced:** 1 June 2026
- **Amount:** $26M Series A (a $13M expansion on top of the earlier raise; total funding to
  date ~$42.6M)
- **Target:** Gradient Labs (London) — vertical AI-agent platform automating regulated

**CORA Group acquires Finastra's U.S. Mid-Market Banking business — M&A**
- **Announced:** 3 June 2026
