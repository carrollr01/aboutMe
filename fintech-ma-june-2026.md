# Fintech M&A and Funding Activity — June 2026

**Scope:** M&A deals and funding rounds where the **target company** operates in one of
three sectors: (1) Asset & Wealth Tech, (2) Capital Markets Tech, (3) Real Estate &
Mortgage Tech.

**Time window:** Deals announced/closed in **June 2026**. Because this list was compiled on
**7 June 2026**, the effective window is **1–7 June 2026**. It will need to be refreshed as
the month progresses.

**Methodology:** Web research across fintech/deal-tracking outlets (FinTech Global,
FinTech Futures, Crunchbase News, BusinessWire/GlobeNewswire press releases, sector trade
press). Every deal below was date-verified to fall inside the window. Several widely-cited
2026 deals that surface in searches but fall **outside** June (e.g. AssetPlus, JP Morgan/
WealthOS, 9fin, Jump, Farther, HabitTrade, CasaPerks, Instant Funding/Funded Trading Plus)
are listed in the "Excluded near-misses" section for transparency.

## 1. Asset & Wealth Tech

### AlphaSense — $350M growth round (funding)
- **Date:** 3 June 2026
- **Type:** Late-stage equity funding round
- **Amount / valuation:** $350M raised; $7.5B post-money valuation (company also reported
  surpassing $600M in ARR)
- **Target:** AlphaSense — AI-powered market intelligence / research platform used by
  asset managers, hedge funds, banks and corporates (buy-side and sell-side research
  workflows). *(Straddles capital markets tech; classified here as asset & wealth tech
  based on its buy-side customer base.)*
- **Investors:** Led by Vitruvian Partners, Accenture Ventures, J.P. Morgan Asset
  Management, D. E. Shaw Ventures and Pinegrove Opportunity Partners; existing backers
  CapitalG, Goldman Sachs Alternatives and Viking Global Investors participated.
- **Sources:**

## 2. Capital Markets Tech

### U.S. Bancorp completes acquisition of BTIG (M&A — closed)
- **Date:** Completed effective 1 June 2026
- **Type:** Strategic acquisition (closed)
- **Amount:** Target consideration up to ~$1B; target purchase price of $725M at closing
  (≈$362.5M cash + ~6.6M U.S. Bancorp shares), with additional contingent/retention
  consideration.
- **Target:** BTIG, LLC — institutional brokerage and capital-markets platform spanning
  institutional equity sales & trading, **equity electronic trading**, equity capital
  markets (ECM) and M&A advisory. *(A broker-dealer rather than a pure software vendor, but
  its electronic-trading platform places it squarely in capital markets tech/infrastructure.)*
- **Acquirer:** U.S. Bancorp (U.S. Bank), expanding its capital markets platform.
- **Sources:**
  - [Yahoo Finance — U.S. Bancorp completes BTIG buyout](https://finance.yahoo.com/markets/stocks/articles/u-bancorp-completes-btig-buyout-173200167.html)
  - [U.S. Bancorp Form 8-K exhibit (SEC)](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/0000036104/000119312526011142/d37749dex991.htm)
