# Fintech M&A and Funding Activity — Announced 30 May – 7 June 2026

**Scope:** M&A deals and funding rounds where the **target company** is a technology
business in one of eight sectors:

1. Asset & Wealth Tech
2. Capital Markets Tech
3. Real Estate & Mortgage Tech
4. Banking & Lending Tech
5. Insurtech
6. Payments Tech
7. Financial Info & Analytics
8. Corporate Financial Function (CFO / spend / treasury / accounting tech)

**Inclusion rules:**
- **Announced 30 May – 7 June 2026** (not merely *closed/completed* in that window from an
  earlier announcement).
- **Funding rounds included only if > $25M.** M&A is included at any deal size.
- Target must itself be an in-scope **technology** company (e.g. acquiring a traditional life
  insurer or a brokerage's people/book does not count as insurtech / capital-markets *tech*).

**Compiled:** 7 June 2026. Note that **30–31 May 2026 fell on a weekend (Sat/Sun)** and
produced no qualifying announcements; in practice the live deals all land **1–7 June**.

**Methodology:** Web research across deal-tracking and trade press (FinTech Global, FinTech
Futures, Crunchbase News, TechCrunch, BusinessWire/GlobeNewswire/PRNewswire releases, sector
outlets). **Every deal was individually date-verified.** A large number of deals that surface
in "first-week-of-June" roundups were actually announced in April/May (or merely closed in
June) and are listed under "Excluded near-misses."

### Real Estate & Mortgage Tech — no qualifying deal in window

30 May – 7 June 2026. The only in-window activity fell **below the $25M funding floor** and is

  [Source](https://fintech.global/2026/06/02/zazume-raises-e2-5m-to-consolidate-spanish-rental-market/)
- **Propsoch** (Bengaluru; real-estate advisory / home-buying) — **$2M** seed, 4 Jun 2026, led
  by Athera Venture Partners.
  [Source](https://news.fundsforngos.org/2026/06/04/bengaluru-proptech-startup-propsoch-raises-2-million-seed-funding-to-accelerate-expansion/)

The nearest *qualifying* real-estate-tech deals all sit just **outside** the window: Clear
Capital's acquisition of computer-vision firm **Restb.ai** (announced 12 May), **Linkhome /
Mortgage One Group** (12 May), **Ownwell** ($50M Series B, 19 Feb), and **OneDome** (£25M,

but it is an insurtech.
